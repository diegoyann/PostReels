1. Scope

This policy describes how information may be handled when PostReels is used to connect a TikTok account and run a video publishing workflow configured by the operator. The actual data flow depends on the workflow, hosting, and third-party services selected by the operator.

2. Information involved

Depending on the permissions granted and workflow configuration, information may include TikTok account identifiers or profile information made available through the authorized connection; OAuth access or refresh credentials needed to make authorized requests; video files; captions and other publishing details; and technical logs needed to operate or troubleshoot the workflow. Do not send sensitive personal information unless it is necessary and you understand how your configured services handle it.

3. How information is used

Information is used to establish the authorized TikTok connection, pass video and publishing details through the configured workflow, submit a publishing request when supported, and maintain or troubleshoot that workflow. PostReels does not claim to publish content without your configuration and authorization.

4. Connected services and sharing

Information may be processed by TikTok and by services you configure, which may include n8n for workflow automation and RunningHub for media processing. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Review your workflow nodes, hosting settings, and provider terms to determine exactly what is sent and where it is stored. The operator does not control third-party processing.

5. Retention and security

Retention depends on the configured hosting, workflow execution history, provider logs, and backups. The operator should set appropriate retention controls in each service. No online service can guarantee absolute security; protect credentials, restrict access to workflow instances, and avoid sharing access tokens.

6. Your choices

You may stop using the workflow and revoke TikTok authorization through TikTok's account settings. Revocation may prevent future workflow runs. It may not remove copies already held in workflow logs or third-party systems; those are subject to the applicable service's controls and retention policies.

7. Children's privacy

PostReels is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children who are not permitted to use the connected services under applicable law or platform rules. If you believe such information was provided, contact the operator so it can be reviewed.
