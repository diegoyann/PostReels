PostReels is a project for connecting video creation and publishing workflows. When you choose to connect TikTok, your configured automation can send a video and its publishing details to your TikTok account.

How data is usedRead the terms ↗
How it works

PostReels is designed to work with user-authorized TikTok OAuth and workflows configured with tools such as n8n and RunningHub. The exact processing, permissions, and storage depend on the workflow and services you configure. Review those settings before connecting an account.
